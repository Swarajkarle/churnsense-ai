# ChurnSense AI — Feature Engineering Dictionary

This document provides a complete reference for all engineered features
generated by the `ChurnFeatureEngineer` class.

**Purpose:** Document every feature transformation so that:
1. The feature engineering logic is transparent and reviewable
2. Stakeholders can understand what each feature represents
3. Reviewers can verify that feature engineering does not introduce data leakage

**Important:**
> Engineered features help the ML model capture patterns. They do NOT imply
> that high/low values of these features cause churn — only that they were
> found useful by the model in training.

---

## Engineered Features

### 1. `tenure_group`
- **Type:** Categorical (ordinal)
- **Formula:** Derived from `tenure` (months)
  - `new`: tenure ≤ 12 months
  - `developing`: 12 < tenure ≤ 24 months
  - `established`: 24 < tenure ≤ 48 months
  - `loyal`: tenure > 48 months
- **Rationale:** Raw tenure is a continuous variable with non-linear effects.
  Customers in their first year are known to have higher churn rates in subscription
  businesses. Binning into lifecycle phases may help models capture this non-linearity.
- **Limitation:** Bin boundaries are heuristic and may not be optimal for all datasets.

---

### 2. `monthly_to_total_ratio`
- **Type:** Numeric
- **Formula:** `MonthlyCharges / max(TotalCharges, 1)`
- **Rationale:** A high ratio may indicate a customer with low tenure relative
  to their charges — i.e., a new, expensive-plan customer. These customers
  may have not yet perceived sufficient value for the cost.
- **Limitation:** This ratio conflates tenure and pricing effects and should be
  interpreted carefully. Computed from raw data — no information from outside
  the training window is used.

---

### 3. `charge_per_tenure_month`
- **Type:** Numeric
- **Formula:** `MonthlyCharges / max(tenure, 1)`
- **Rationale:** Normalizes the monthly charge by tenure to capture the average
  cost the customer experiences per month of their subscription.
- **Limitation:** For short-tenure customers, this may produce unstable estimates.

---

### 4. `service_count`
- **Type:** Numeric (integer)
- **Formula:** Count of columns in `[PhoneService, OnlineSecurity, OnlineBackup,
  DeviceProtection, TechSupport, StreamingTV, StreamingMovies]`
  that have the value `"Yes"`.
- **Rationale:** Customers subscribed to more services have higher switching costs
  and likely perceive higher value — both are correlated with lower churn in
  subscription businesses.
- **Limitation:** All services are treated as equally weighted, which may not
  reflect actual perceived value differences.

---

### 5. `is_month_to_month`
- **Type:** Binary (0/1)
- **Formula:** 1 if `Contract == "Month-to-month"`, else 0
- **Rationale:** Month-to-month contracts have no lock-in period. They are
  consistently identified as a strong predictor of churn in subscription
  domain literature.
- **Limitation:** Binary encoding ignores the intermediate case (One year).

---

### 6. `is_two_year`
- **Type:** Binary (0/1)
- **Formula:** 1 if `Contract == "Two year"`, else 0
- **Rationale:** Two-year contracts represent the highest lock-in and are
  typically associated with very low churn probability.

---

### 7. `is_fiber`
- **Type:** Binary (0/1)
- **Formula:** 1 if `InternetService == "Fiber optic"`, else 0
- **Rationale:** Fiber optic internet is more expensive and, in some datasets,
  is associated with higher churn due to price sensitivity. This feature
  isolates the fiber effect.

---

### 8. `is_electronic_check`
- **Type:** Binary (0/1)
- **Formula:** 1 if `PaymentMethod == "Electronic check"`, else 0
- **Rationale:** Electronic check is a payment method associated with higher
  churn rates in telecom datasets, potentially due to less financial commitment
  than automatic payment methods.
- **Limitation:** This correlation may be dataset-specific and should not be
  generalized without domain validation.

---

### 9. `has_no_internet`
- **Type:** Binary (0/1)
- **Formula:** 1 if `InternetService == "No"`, else 0
- **Rationale:** Customers without internet service have a significantly different
  product footprint and different churn dynamics than internet subscribers.

---

### 10. `high_value_customer` *(conditional)*
- **Type:** Binary (0/1)
- **Formula:** 1 if `MonthlyCharges > 70` AND `tenure > 24`, else 0
- **Rationale:** Identifies customers who have been loyal and pay premium prices.
  These customers are valuable and may have different retention characteristics.
- **Limitation:** Threshold of 70 and 24 months are heuristic — not derived
  from data — and should be reviewed for each specific business context.

## Preprocessing After Feature Engineering

After feature engineering, the `ChurnPreprocessor` applies:

| Step | Applied To | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **Imputation** | Numeric | Median (robust to outliers) |
| **Imputation** | Categorical | Constant ("missing") |
| **Scaling** | Numeric | StandardScaler (zero mean, unit variance) |
| **Encoding** | Categorical | OneHotEncoder (drop first to avoid multicollinearity) |

The preprocessor is **fitted on training data only** and applied identically
to test/inference data. This prevents data leakage.

The preprocessor is serialized with `joblib.dump()` and must be the same
object used at inference time.

---

## Data Leakage Prevention

1. `TotalCharges` is calculated from actual billed amounts — not from any
   future-period data. It is a legitimate input feature.
2. `tenure` at prediction time reflects tenure at the time of the prediction
   call, not future tenure.
3. The target column `Churn` is removed from the feature matrix before
   preprocessing begins.
4. The preprocessor is fitted only on training rows, not on the test split.
5. Engineered features that are derived from multiple columns use only the
   values in the current row — no cross-row aggregation.
